Navigate the world, capital by capital
🎯 The Goal

Travel from your starting capital city to the target — the red dot on the globe — in as few shots as possible.

🏌️ Taking a Shot
1Pick a club based on how far you want to travel
2Type a capital city as your next stop
3Hit Shoot — match the club to the distance to avoid a penalty
🏒 Clubs & Distance

Each club has an ideal range. Using the wrong club earns a penalty stroke.

Putter800 km
9-Iron1,600 km
· · ·
Driver8,000 km
⚠️ Penalties

You earn a +1 penalty stroke in two situations:

🏒
Wrong club — the club you selected doesn't match the distance to your chosen city. Each club has a specific range; if the shot distance falls outside that range, it counts as a penalty. The game will tell you which club was ideal.
💨
Overshoot — see below.
💨 Overshooting

If the city you pick is further from you than the target is, your shot flies past the hole and lands somewhere beyond it — just like hitting a putt too hard.

📍You are in Berlin
🎯Target is Cairo (3,200 km away)
✈️You shoot to Nairobi (6,100 km away)
💥Overshot! You land in a random city past Cairo — +1 penalty

Always pick a city that keeps you between your current position and the target, or land exactly on the target to finish the hole.
